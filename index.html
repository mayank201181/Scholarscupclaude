The Four Events

The World Scholar's Cup has four events. Every team competes in all four. Your total score determines individual and team medals and trophies.

How the Day Flows

  1. Opening Ceremony – the host introduces the curriculum theme and welcomes teams.
  2. Team Debate – three rounds, often the first competitive event.
  3. Collaborative Writing – your team picks one of six prompts, then writes individually.
  4. Scholar's Challenge – 120 multiple-choice questions across six subjects.
  5. Scholar's Bowl – the multimedia team event in the dark room.
  6. Scholars Scholarship / Debate Showcase / Closing Ceremony – medals, alpacas, and the Cupcake Battle.
